The *Anthropie Working Papers* (AWP) constitute the academic corpus of the framework of anthropy. Each paper is deposited on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/anthropie-working-papers) with a permanent DOI.

Each paper is published under a CC-BY 4.0 licence, with an English version carrying its own DOI and citation exports (BibTeX, RIS, EndNote). The framework formalised here is developed in the book [*ANTHROPIE — Ordre ici. Dette ailleurs*](/livres/anthropie-ordre-ici-dette-ailleurs/) (in French).
