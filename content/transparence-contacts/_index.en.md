This page covers three uses: [professional outreach](#contacts-professionnels), the
[“Free resources” scheme](#ressources-offertes) and [image usage
rights](#droits-images).

## Why you may have received a message {#contacts-professionnels}

In connection with my books, articles and *Anthropie Working Papers*, I sometimes
write individually to professionals — journalists, researchers, teachers, editorial
managers, elected officials or institutions — when their activity has a direct link
with the text, the data, the resource or the book I am pointing to.

These messages are sent in small numbers, with no purchased lists and no mass mailing.

## Data controller

Stéphane Lalut, economist — independent researcher.
ORCID: [0009-0002-1794-4895](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-1794-4895).

You can contact me by replying to the message you received or through the
[Contact page](/en/contact/) of the site.

## Data used and sources

Only the professional information needed to make contact is used: identity, role,
institution, published professional address, work or activities related to the
message, and a minimal record of exchanges.

This information comes from publicly accessible professional sources: institutional
websites, media, universities, laboratories, journals, official directories or signed
publications.

No private address is sought, bought or inferred from an assumed convention.

## Purposes and legal basis

This data is used solely to send individualised professional correspondence: an
editorial proposal, a press copy, the flagging of documented work, a scientific
exchange or the sharing of a resource.

The processing rests on my legitimate interest in making known work that may be
useful to the recipient's professional activity, in accordance with Article 6(1)(f)
of the GDPR (the French *RGPD*).

No decision producing a legal or similarly significant effect is taken automatically.
The data is neither sold nor passed on to third parties for their own purposes.

## Retention

Preparatory files are deleted after 90 days at the latest. The minimal record of
exchanges is kept for as long as is needed to follow them up.

In the event of an objection, minimised information is kept for at least three
years, solely in order to prevent any further contact.

## Your rights

You may request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, or restriction of
its processing.

You may object at any time to further contact. A simple reply saying “do not contact
me again” is enough. A minimal piece of data may then be kept exclusively in order to
comply with this request.

You can exercise your rights by replying to the message you received or through the
[Contact page](/en/contact/). You may also lodge a complaint with the
[CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr) (the French data protection authority).

## “Free books” scheme {#ressources-offertes}

When you request a free copy on the Free books page, a random identifier is stored in
your browser (until the site's data is deleted) in order to prevent several copies
from being allocated. When a copy is allocated, this identifier and the book chosen
are kept on our technical infrastructure (Cloudflare) for the duration of the scheme,
then deleted no later than three months after it closes. This identifier contains
neither your name nor your email address. It is a measure strictly necessary to
provide the service requested; your rights are exercised as indicated above.
